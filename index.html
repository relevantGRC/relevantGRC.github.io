Profile

I’m a senior Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) analyst with deep expertise in building and scaling enterprise security and compliance programs across research, federal, and emerging AI-driven environments. With extensive hands-on experience in frameworks like ISO 27001, ISO 27701, SOC 2, NIST, and evolving standards such as ISO/IEC 42001, I specialize in translating complex regulatory requirements into practical, measurable, and audit-ready solutions.

My strength lies in designing and operationalizing end-to-end management systems — from ISMS implementation and control design to metrics development, internal audits, and continuous improvement. I’ve led initiatives to mature security awareness programs, develop compliance dashboards, and integrate governance into modern development ecosystems using approaches like Policy as Code. What sets me apart: I don’t treat compliance as a checkbox exercise. I focus on building sustainable, scalable programs that align security with business objectives.

Whether it’s developing executive-level reporting, enabling teams through clear processes and training, or aligning cross-functional stakeholders, I bring both strategic vision and execution discipline. I bridge the gap between technical teams, leadership, and auditors, delivering clarity, trust, and measurable security outcomes in complex, high-stakes environments.
